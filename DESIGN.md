# CLACK Visual Direction

## Subject

A physical receipt printer and a working treasury counter, not a robot or an investment landing page. The first screen is an inspectable workstation. Each view supports a distinct job: observation, allocation, evidence and methodology.

## Tokens

- Counter surface: `#eeeff1`
- Machine ground: `#e4e6ea`
- Receipt paper: `#fafaf6`
- Ink and masthead: `#24262b`
- Active control blue: `#3659d9`
- Physical confirmation key: `#eb986c`
- Type: Silkscreen for the identity and short display titles; IBM Plex Mono for records and controls.

The memorable asset is the receipt machine. Content uses flat bands, open rows and restrained lines; a physical receipt is the deliberate framed exception. Color separates materials and roles, not a monochrome glow. Body text and controls stay quiet.

## Motion

The opening feeds pixel paper upward before the sheet expands into the page. It is native Canvas, never an embedded video. Verification drives a short feed animation; the process log comes from completed server operations. No artificial transaction ticker. Reduced-motion visitors skip the opening, and hidden tabs suspend canvas work.

## Deliverables

`src/art.mjs` is the shared artwork source for the site, PNG identity assets, banner, post stills and three eight-second videos. The mark remains inspectable and crisp at a large size. Desktop and mobile share the same object but use different receipt arrangements. No marketing-kit links in the public navigation.
