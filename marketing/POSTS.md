## Post 2 / Settlement

A prepared payment is not a paid payment.

CLACK divides a SOL amount into exact recipient allocations. The treasury wallet signs the batch. Its receipt is finalized only when the payer, recipients, amounts and unique batch reference match the chain.

No amount disappears into rounding. Network fees remain separate.
